# How to take this course

This course assumes **no trading background**. Start with [Volume 1, Module 1](LESSONS_01_FOUNDATIONS.md), which explains stocks and markets, then complete [the first seven days](ADVANCED_FIRST_WEEK.md) as its first practice week. Continue the teaching volumes in order. [COURSE.md](COURSE.md) is the map; the **lesson volumes** contain the explanations and exercises. Most weeks have ten hours of work. If a topic takes longer, stay with it.

Before assigning later weeks, take the [arithmetic and math diagnostic](FOUNDATION_MATH_BRIDGE.md) and record the first attempt in a copy of [the learning record](LEARNING_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md). A learner who already codes can bring Python into the first payoff lesson and demonstrate Module 15's programming skill early. Use the saved study time for options, probability, and contract arithmetic. Programming experience never substitutes for those gates. The [three goal paths](MASTERY_TRACKS.md) become projects after the common core; you may pursue all three in sequence.

## The learning loop

1. **Learn one idea.** Read one section of the current lesson and say it back in ordinary language. If a term cannot be explained without using three more unfamiliar terms, pause and look it up.
2. **Work an example by hand.** Cover the worked solution. Write the input, units, formula or reasoning, result, and what the result means. Use dollars, shares, contracts, percentage points, and dates explicitly.
3. **Change one assumption.** For a payoff example, change the stock price or premium. For a research example, change the fee or fill. Ask whether the conclusion survives.
4. **Attempt exercises before the key.** Record an answer even if uncertain. Then check the separate solution and write the exact step you missed. Re-solve a fresh problem a day later without the key. Use the [foundation assessment bank](FOUNDATION_ASSESSMENTS.md) for unfamiliar cases.
5. **Build the module artifact.** A chart, ledger, memo, or case analysis is evidence of skill. Watching the videos is only preparation.
6. **Pass the gate.** The numerical result and the explanation must both be right. Safety-critical mistakes about contract size, cash obligations, assignment, and settlement require a new correct attempt before progressing. Preserve the prompt, original answer, correction, and retest in [the learning record](LEARNING_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md).

An option may be complex without being mysterious. Draw its cash flows, ask what can happen before expiration, and check the exact contract terms. The current [OCC disclosure document](https://www.theocc.com/company-information/documents-and-archives/options-disclosure-document) and broker procedures are references whenever the case concerns a real product.

## Use me as your tutor

You can work through the material in this chat. Ask **“Teach me Week 1, Day 1”** and I should explain one concept at a time, show a simple example, ask you to solve a new example, and respond to your reasoning. When you are ready, ask **“Quiz me on Module 1 without showing answers.”** Send your answers; I should mark each one, explain misses, and give fresh cases before advancing. Ask **“Review my module artifact”** to get feedback against its pass gate.

Do not copy an answer key into your response to pass a gate. Being able to solve an unseen version matters more than remembering a number. The answer key is for feedback, not a score to collect.

For a deeper review, ask **“Give me a fresh assignment case with an account balance and broker deadline; wait for my full ledger.”** For research, ask **“Challenge the timing, costs, and benchmark of my dated protocol.”** Record the answers and changes in your own files; a chat response by itself is not an enduring research record.

## Weekly record

Use [the full learning record template](LEARNING_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md) for assessment attempts, artifacts, and gate decisions. At minimum, copy this block into a dated journal file each week:

```text
Week / module:
Hours studied (target 10; actual):
Concepts I can explain without notes:
Worked examples attempted:
Exercises correct on first attempt:
Fresh retest attempted, identifier, and result:
Errors and corrected reasoning:
One option-chain or market observation, with source and timestamp:
What I still do not understand:
Module artifact progress:
Artifact file path, input version, and independent check:
Any paper decision and its prewritten reason (later modules only):
Any no-trade decision or unfilled order (later modules only):
Next week's focus:
```

For market observations, a delayed quote is suitable for learning but not a promise of an executable price. Record the displayed bid and ask, not only the midpoint. [OIC labels its tool data as delayed](https://www.optionseducation.org/options-quotes-calculators).
