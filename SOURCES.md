# Options trading course: source library

Reviewed October 26, 2026. This is a curated reading and practice list for the course, not a claim to cover every course, blog, or book online. Start with the free resources. Paid texts and historical option data are optional until the learner reaches the research stages. Prices, access terms, broker procedures, and rules can change; verify them at the linked source when used.

## How sources were selected

1. **Mechanics and risk:** Prefer the SEC, OCC, FINRA, exchanges, and original product specifications over influencer summaries.
2. **Learning:** Prefer coherent lessons with exercises, quizzes, or simulations. A strategy description alone does not establish that it makes money.
3. **Research:** Prefer original academic papers and datasets with documented fields, timestamps, adjustment rules, and licensing.
4. **Claims about performance:** Require executable assumptions, bid/ask and fees, out-of-sample tests, and drawdowns. Treat testimonials, win rates, and backtests without these as ideas to test.
5. **Current details:** Recheck disclosure documents, margin and broker policies, product settlement, and data specifications before live use. Historical textbooks explain models, not current brokerage rules.

## Practice supplied with this course

Use the [math bridge](FOUNDATION_MATH_BRIDGE.md) and [separate assessment and answer files](FOUNDATION_ASSESSMENTS.md) before buying another introductory course. The [operational casebook](OPERATIONAL_CASEBOOK.md) supplies account and assignment scenarios; [execution practice](EXECUTION_PRACTICE.md) supplies timestamped order decisions; the [dynamic hedging lab](DYNAMIC_HEDGING_LAB.md) reconciles an option and stock hedge. The [Python labs](labs/README.md) provide small **synthetic** data and runnable accounting, data-quality, execution, and research checks. Synthetic data test your process and software; they cannot establish a live trading edge. Record first attempts and corrections in [the learning record](LEARNING_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md).

Before acquiring historical option quotes, complete the feasibility screen in [the research lab](RESEARCH_LAB.md): exact contract universe, fields, timestamps, corporate-action treatment, license, cost, and the study that the data can support. Published index history, a delayed chain, and licensed contract-level quotes answer different questions. The [three later paths](MASTERY_TRACKS.md) set different project standards for personal portfolio decisions, independent research, and professional preparation.

## Data and primary research for the advanced stage

| Source | Research use and caveat |
| --- | --- |
| [Cboe historical VIX and volatility indices](https://www.cboe.com/tradable_products/vix/vix_historical_data) | Free daily index history for volatility context. VIX is an index, not a complete history of executable option quotes. |
| [OCC volume query](https://www.theocc.com/market-data/market-data-reports/volume-and-open-interest/volume-query), [open-interest reports](https://www.theocc.com/market-data/market-data-reports/volume-and-open-interest/open-interest), and [information memos](https://infomemo.theocc.com/infomemo/search-memo) | Official options volume and open-interest reports; memos document contract adjustments and special deliverables after corporate actions. Confirm each report's available date range before designing a study. |
| [SEC EDGAR filings](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings) | Free issuer filings for earnings and corporate-event research; record the filing timestamp to avoid look-ahead bias. |
| [FRED SOFR](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/SOFR) and [3-month Treasury yield](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS3MO) | Public interest-rate series. Align publication and observation dates with the simulated decision time; cite source and transformations. |
| [Cboe DataShop option quote intervals](https://datashop.cboe.com/option-quote-intervals) | Historical NBBO bid/ask, sizes, volume, and optional Greeks for U.S. listed equity, ETF, and index options. This is a paid dataset; confirm exact fields, timestamps, coverage, licensing, and costs before buying. It does not cover options on futures. Cboe says its quote-size capture changed on June 22, 2026, which matters for historical liquidity comparisons. |
| [OptionMetrics IvyDB US](https://optionmetrics.com/united-states/) | Research-grade daily option bids/asks, volume, open interest, Greeks, volatility surfaces, and corporate-action handling. Commercial/institutional access; inspect methodology and survivorship coverage. |
| [Bakshi and Kapadia, 2003, *Delta-Hedged Gains and the Negative Market Volatility Risk Premium*](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/16/2/527/1579962) | Original empirical study connecting delta-hedged option returns and volatility risk. Historical finding to replicate and stress-test, not a current trade recommendation. |
| [Tian and Wu, 2023, *Limits of Arbitrage and Primary Risk-Taking in Derivative Securities*](https://academic.oup.com/raps/article-abstract/13/3/405/7035950) | Delta hedging reduces, but does not eliminate, risk; useful for decomposing the risks left in an options strategy. |
| [Bailey et al., *The Probability of Backtest Overfitting*](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2326253) | Original research on strategy selection bias and why repeated backtest searches can produce fragile winners. Use to design a fixed test protocol and holdout evaluation. |

## Review cadence

Check SEC, FINRA, OCC, exchange product specifications, broker permissions, and data-vendor documentation before every new live-product module. In particular, [FINRA's 2026 intraday margin transition guidance](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/intraday-margin-requirements) says firms may remain on older day-trading requirements during the transition through October 20, 2027; confirm the rule actually applied by the learner's broker. Review this source library at least quarterly and record newly verified dates and replacement links.

## Verification boundary

The public source descriptions and links above were rechecked on October 26, 2026. This review did not log into the Cboe learning portal or a broker, execute a simulator order, or purchase a historical data product. Tool features, course access, historical coverage, and trade fills should be confirmed inside the relevant account before relying on them. Some publisher and academic pages restrict automated access; their bibliographic details were cross-checked against the publisher's or journal's indexed page.
