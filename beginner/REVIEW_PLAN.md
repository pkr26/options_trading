# A flexible plan for 84 small sessions

[Beginner course](README.md) · [Workbook](WORKBOOK.md) · [Full library](../README.md)

The planning baseline is **64 new-lesson sessions + 8 checkpoint sessions + 12 review sessions = 84 sessions**. At **five 10–15 minute sessions per week**, that is 16 full weeks plus 4 sessions, or **about 17 weeks**. Retries, rest days, split lessons, and capstone work can add sessions. There is no completion deadline or promised date of trading readiness.

On a lesson day, allow roughly 5–10 minutes to read, reproduce the example, and answer; use 3–5 minutes for recall. Stop at your time limit and resume where you left off. The four small capstone cases in Lesson 63 may need separate sessions. Split any checkpoint taking longer than 15 minutes; keep the saved first attempt and continue the next day.

## Make reviews spaced

At the start or end of each study session, spend a minute recalling one earlier idea without notes. Revisit a lesson approximately **1, 3, and 7 study days** after learning it; exact spacing can shift around your schedule. These brief recalls are inside the daily time budget. The 12 dedicated review sessions provide longer cumulative practice.

For a dedicated review, choose three questions: one from recent lessons, one older question, and one item you previously missed. Change at least one number in a calculation. Explain units and cash/share changes. Check only after preserving your answer. Spend the remaining time correcting one weak idea; avoid rereading an entire module passively.

Example after Lesson 20: recall why a bid differs from an ask, calculate a new 100-multiplier premium, then solve a fresh put-profit case. This combines old and recent knowledge.

## Advance when the idea is clear

Each module's checkpoint gives its specific standard. For a missed concept, reread the relevant lesson and solve a changed case next study day. Correct every cash, multiplier, share, exercise/assignment, expiration, and order-status error before moving to material that depends on it. Keep original answers and later retries distinct in the [workbook](WORKBOOK.md).

If ordinary percentages or dollar units remain difficult, pause for the relevant portion of the [math bridge](../FOUNDATION_MATH_BRIDGE.md). If a lesson feels easy, still answer its fresh question and explain why the answer follows. Coding and advanced pricing formulas are optional later study.

## After the final session

Choose a small next step: repeat weak lessons, observe dated chains without orders, keep a paper decision log, study a particular portfolio question, or begin an optional deeper module. A decision not to trade is valid. Course completion, checkpoint scores, and simulated gains do not provide broker approval, personal suitability, or proof of a live edge.
