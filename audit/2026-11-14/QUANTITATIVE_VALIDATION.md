## Precision and interpretation observations

- The largest price discrepancy occurred at spot/strike $10,000, ten years, 20% volatility, −10% continuous rate and zero yield. It is $0.00247078/share, approximately $0.247078 per 100-share contract. The normal CDF is an approximation, and displayed cent rounding should not be presented as an exact external market reference.
- In the 432-case derivative sweep, maximum absolute delta difference was 0.00134294 and gamma difference 0.02075463. Both arose in one-day/1% volatility inputs; fixed-step finite differences can be sensitive around narrow payoff transitions. Maximum vega difference was 0.0000134944 per share per IV point, and one-day time-change difference 0.0000138028/share. These observations support the existing local-approximation caveats; no exact-Greek claim is warranted.
- Collateral reservations remain a separate liquidity constraint. They are not deducted again as a trading loss in the stress model. The supplied gross cash obligation must be interpreted separately from the marked P&L to avoid double counting.
- Long option marks in the assignment ledger are supplied accounting inputs. The ledger correctly computes equity from those inputs but does not independently establish that every user-entered mark is economically coherent or executable.
- All 26 original case briefs explicitly label fictional teaching data. Each package has at least one source link. This audit sampled primary-source support for specific contract concepts; it did not exhaustively verify every external link or every inherited teaching paragraph.

## Primary-source checks

- Assignment on one spread leg does not automatically exercise the other leg, and remaining positions/funding require action: [OIC, What happens if one leg is assigned?](https://www.optionseducation.org/getcontentasset/86b32e5f-c816-4a0c-968f-5b9a36b6363f/dfc3d011-8f63-43f6-9ed8-4b444333a1d0/understandingassignment_1.pdf).
- A collar combines long stock and put protection with a short call and a cap on upside: [OIC, Collar](https://prd-web.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/collar-protective-collar).
- Corporate actions can change contract deliverable and multiplier; no universal adjusted-share-count assumption is justified: [OIC, Options Glossary](https://prd-web.optionseducation.org/referencelibrary/optionsglossary).
- Extrinsic/time value is the premium beyond intrinsic value before expiration; time value is zero at expiration: [OIC, Options Pricing](https://prd-web.optionseducation.org/optionsoverview/options-pricing), [CME, Calculating Options Moneyness and Intrinsic Value](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/introduction-to-options/calculating-options-moneyness-and-intrinsic-value).

The source descriptions above are paraphrases. They support the named concepts, not endorsement of this course.

## What this audit cannot establish

The calculations use stipulated fictional prices, model assumptions and episode fills. They do not validate broker-specific margin, assignment deadlines, historical execution, suitability, strategy profitability or future returns. Correct calculations and software tests cannot establish that a learner understands the ideas, that a human reviewer accepts the curriculum, or that the teaching improves retention. Genuine learners, elapsed follow-up time and actual knowledgeable reviewers remain necessary for those claims.
