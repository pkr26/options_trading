# Your first options course session

**Today takes 10–15 minutes. You do not need to know anything about trading.** Your only task is [Lesson 01 in Money and markets](beginner/01_MARKETS.md).

Read **only Lesson 01**. Its idea is that your first goal is understanding. Work through its example, write your own answer to its one question, then open the answer. You may stop there.

## Your daily routine

| Minutes | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 | Recall yesterday's one idea without looking. On your first day, write why you want to learn. |
| 2–8 | Read one lesson and follow its worked example. |
| 8–12 | Answer the new question before revealing the solution. |
| 12–15 | Correct one mistake and write the idea in your own words. |

Some lessons need two sessions. This is expected. There is no deadline and no requirement to trade.

## A glimpse of Lesson 02

A **share** is a small ownership interest in a company. If you buy two shares at $10 each, the shares cost $20 before fees. If their market price later falls to $8 each, they are worth $16 together. The price can move in either direction. This ownership idea comes before learning the contracts called options. [The SEC explains stocks](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/stocks).

You do not have to study calls, puts, Greeks or strategies today. Those arrive in small steps after the basic market lessons.
