Confirmed findings

A1 — Medium: later research-path standards do not branch by evidence level.
Locations: MASTERY_TRACKS.md:26–29 (especially line 28), compared with
RESEARCH_LAB.md:61,69,75,95 and LESSONS_04_RESEARCH.md:141,177.
The later research path explicitly accepts synthetic, published-index or licensed
contract evidence in item 2, then unconditionally requires a trade/portfolio ledger,
realistic quote-side/adverse fills, assignments and later frozen-rule evaluation.
Published-index evidence cannot reconstruct those missing constituent-level fills;
the lab and Volume 4 explicitly replace those tasks for level B. A learner following
the later-path checklist alone can be asked to fabricate unobservable data or fail
a path that otherwise accepts their evidence level.
Fix: branch items 3–4 into A simulator cases, B source/index reconciliation and
new-publication tracking, C contract ledger/execution/forward shadow evaluation,
using the same standards as Volume 4 and the lab. Keep any separate hypothetical
operational exercise explicitly labeled as such.

A2 — Low/medium: the map promises calendar no-arbitrage checks without a lesson.
Locations: COURSE.md:84 versus LESSONS_04_RESEARCH.md:17–21,41.
Module 19 lists calendar/butterfly arbitrage checks. The lesson shows a positive
forward-variance calculation and same-expiry call-strike monotonicity/convexity,
but does not teach a calendar-check condition, a violating two-expiry example,
or an assessment for this promised skill. A repository search found no separate
calendar-arbitrage lesson or checker. Referring to advanced surface research at
the end of the lab does not supply the missing worked teaching step.
Fix: add a qualified European two-expiry test and broken-input example with
explicit rates/dividends/forward-moneyness assumptions, plus a pass task; or
narrow the map's scope to the strike checks actually taught. Do not teach an
unqualified rule that any lower annualized IV at a later expiry is arbitrage.

A3 — Low: map's zero-quote rejection instruction conflicts with detailed QA.
Locations: COURSE.md:70 versus LESSONS_03_QUANT.md:215–217,221 and
RESEARCH_LAB.md:53.
The map says "Reject crossed/zero/stale quotes". The detailed lesson correctly
says a zero-bid quote may be real and should be flagged to determine whether
an exit was possible, with written rules and exclusion counts. A blanket zero-bid
deletion can remove the evidence of illiquidity that a loss/exit study needs.
Fix: say reject impossible quotes; flag/investigate crossed, stale and zero-bid
conditions under prespecified rules; preserve held positions and unresolved exits.
This finding concerns conflicting summaries, not evidence that the current lab
silently erases positions.

Optional improvements, not counted as confirmed defects
- Probability introduction at LESSONS_03_QUANT.md:15 equates probability zero
  with impossibility. That is a convenient finite-coin simplification; for the
  later continuous-price models explain that individual exact outcomes can
  have zero probability without being impossible.
- Specify a prerequisite completion/diagnostic for plotting, numerical methods
  and multivariable calculus before professional-quant extensions. The current
  text already allows timeline extension and does not promise job readiness.
- Trial the course with learners and record actual time, misconception frequency
  and fresh-test retention; current pacing and mastery thresholds are design
  choices rather than empirically established effectiveness claims.
