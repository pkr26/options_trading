The built-in numerical pilot uses its server baseline timestamp. Day 7 opens at elapsed day 7 and closes at day 14; day 30 opens at day 30 and closes at day 45. Its actual completion time and missed state must remain visible. For separately administered written forms, freeze the windows in advance and record actual elapsed time. For each written stage, save the initial memo, cash/scenario working, the timestamp at which the update was released, revised memo and defense response as separate versioned evidence. Record whether a tutor, worked solution, another person, calculator, code or published key was used. A later correction is useful learning evidence but does not replace the original attempt.

Two raters independently inspect the prespecified shared subset. Each receives the exact case packet and the learner's saved initial/revised work, with identity, learning-stage labels, earlier scores and the other rater's scores hidden where feasible.
