## Review question and scope

Does this course provide accurate, coherent, teachable and assessable preparation for explaining options mechanics and making defensible portfolio or research decisions? Assess the specific materials inspected. “MBA-style” describes the intended teaching standard; the review does not award academic credit or accreditation.

The course contains a 64-lesson beginner route and 26 advanced teaching packages. The advanced pathway progresses from contracts and account decisions, through price behavior and execution, to quantitative methods, research and portfolio risk. Module 10, **The Meridian Investment Committee**, is the integrated Trade Design seminar. It requires a recommendation, a common-resource portfolio comparison, a response to changed information and a defense.

Review the [curriculum map](COURSE.md), [teaching standard](GRADUATE_TEACHING_STANDARD.md), [learner pilot protocol](GRADUATE_PILOT_PROTOCOL.md), [parallel written case forms](PILOT_PARALLEL_CASES.md), and [rating/report template](PILOT_RATING_AND_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md). The four teaching volumes and their primary references supply the substantive lessons. The app's reference library and offline course ZIP contain the teaching materials; this source checkout also contains `course-app/content/graduate.json` and the implementation.

The live app supports submitted case revisions, numerical practice, deterministic tutoring, formative text checks and attributed review records. ## Review method

1. Freeze the exact course/source version, case packet and reference dates. Record the files or app views inspected.
2. Inspect every module's objective, prerequisite, taught calculation, case, rubric and transfer prompt. Record coverage at module level; do not extrapolate a sample inspection into approval of all 26 modules.
3. Independently reconstruct the flagship's cash balances, scenario table, severe boundary and revised feasibility decisions. Check all new pilot form tables before administering them.
4. Inspect contract mechanics, lifecycle and funding in detail. Then inspect model scope, execution assumptions and research interpretation with appropriate specialists where needed.
5. Assess representative learner work using the four-dimension rubric below. Preserve quotations and calculation checks supporting each score.
6. Resolve critical findings, rerun the affected exercises, and record the reviewer’s actual final decision. Unresolved items remain visible.

Software verification evidence can inform the review, but the reviewer should inspect its coverage and limitations. A passing test is not proof that the explanation teaches well.

## Content review checklist

Mark checked only after actual inspection. A checked box means the item was examined; the findings log carries the judgment.

- [ ] Rights and obligations, premium units, deliverables and exercise cash are distinguished correctly.
- [ ] Closing, exercise, assignment, settlement and remaining positions are reconciled as separate events.
- [ ] Adjusted-contract terms are supplied explicitly; a premium multiplier is not used to infer an unspecified exercise obligation.
- [ ] Cash deadlines, collateral, marks, P&L and terminal loss are separated.
- [ ] Portfolio alternatives use the same starting resources, dates and economic objective, including retained cash and stated costs.
- [ ] Quote sides, stated fills, cancellations and unfilled orders are accounted for without invented midpoint execution.
- [ ] Model prices, local sensitivity estimates, expiration values and executable prices have distinct labels.
- [ ] Greeks, volatility and probability conventions are stated, including the limits of local approximations and model pricing weights.
- [ ] Data timestamps, missing exits, held positions, trial counts, comparator choice and dependence are treated honestly.
- [ ] Synthetic, published-series and historical-contract evidence support different, explicitly limited conclusions.
- [ ] Source references are primary, relevant, dated where needed, and do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
- [ ] Recommendations follow a stated mandate and uncertainty; profit is not used as a substitute for reasoning quality.

## Analytic rubric for written case work

Use **0–3 per dimension**, matching the current app review desk. A missing score is blank, not zero. Record an exact quotation or a reproducible calculation as evidence. A sum out of 12 is descriptive; unresolved contract or funding errors cannot be compensated for by polished prose.

| Dimension | 0: missing or materially wrong | 1: partial or insufficiently defended | 2: supported with stated limitations | 3: reproducible, challenged and defended |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contract, units and calculation | Material unit, obligation, position or cash result is wrong or absent. | Some working is correct, but missing steps or an unresolved error prevent reproduction. | Correct contract terms and principal cash/scenario calculations are shown, with relevant assumptions and limits. | A second reader can reproduce the complete ledger; boundary/adverse cases and changed-input checks support the result. |
| Objective and alternatives | No usable mandate or fair comparison; a strategy label substitutes for a decision. | An objective and alternative are named, but the comparison omits a material tradeoff or changes resources. | A recommendation follows the stated objective and compares a relevant alternative under common resources. | The strongest opposing alternative is fairly represented, and the conditions favoring either choice are defended. |
| Risk and evidence | Unsupported certainty, concealed assumptions, or missing funding/operational exposure changes the conclusion. | Risk categories are named without showing their consequence, or evidence scope is overstated. | Known facts, assumptions, costs, uncertainty and relevant operational limits are distinguished. | A consequential adverse scenario and invalidation condition are tested; source/evidence limits constrain the conclusion. |
| Revision and communication | The first answer is missing or the update is ignored or silently substituted. | A revision is asserted with weak causal explanation or an unclear audit trail. | Initial and revised views are preserved and the update produces an explained revision or defended continuation. | A concise defense addresses a serious challenge, reproduces its numerical effect and identifies any remaining uncertainty. |

**Critical flags:** wrong deliverable or exercise cash; unexplained missing position; invented fill; failure to meet a stated cash deadline; use of future information; unreported tried variants; an unsupported guarantee. Identify the exact flag and affected conclusion. Require remediation and a changed follow-up case. The reviewer may still score other dimensions, while leaving acceptance pending.

## Blinded paired rating and calibration

Use two knowledgeable raters for a shared calibration subset. Give each a pseudonymous answer packet with its exact case assumptions, but hide the learner's identity, stage label, earlier scores and the other rater's scores. Retain form identity where it is needed to apply the correct key. Do not hide a material contract term to create artificial blindness.

Each rater independently records four scores, critical flags, evidence quotations and one challenge. Freeze those original ratings before discussion. Reconcile any difference greater than one point in a dimension, any disagreement about a critical flag, or any disagreement about acceptance. Record both originals and an adjudicated interpretation. Do not overwrite disagreement to make agreement appear higher. Use known annotated course submissions for calibration practice; they are not independent participant outcomes.
