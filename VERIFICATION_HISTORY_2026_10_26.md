# Independent verification of the options course

**Checked:** October 26, 2026. **Scope:** [README.md](README.md), [START_HERE.md](START_HERE.md), [COURSE.md](COURSE.md), [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md), [RISK_AND_OPERATIONS.md](RISK_AND_OPERATIONS.md), and [RESEARCH_LAB.md](RESEARCH_LAB.md). This report records a second review of factual claims, source fit, worked arithmetic, and internal consistency. The 104-week schedule and grading thresholds are curriculum design choices, not empirically proven routes to elite performance.

## Verification method

We re-read the materials against current pages from the SEC, OCC/OIC, FINRA, Cboe, CME, IBKR, IRS, MIT, the relevant publishers and data vendors, and the abstracts or texts of cited original research. We checked the option payoff calculations independently, mapped the curriculum's prerequisites, and verified all Markdown file references. The project has 70 distinct external URLs across the six original documents and 14 internal document links; all 14 internal links resolve. Public source descriptions were checked against their providers. Account-only features and licensed historical data were not represented as directly tested.

## What the second review found

| Area | Verification result and correction |
| --- | --- |
| Beginner contract description | The initial lesson described share delivery too broadly. It now says its examples are **standard physically settled stock options**. [OIC distinguishes these from cash-settled index options](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/equity-vs-index-options); the [OCC disclosure document](https://www.theocc.com/company-information/documents-and-archives/options-disclosure-document) also distinguishes physical delivery and cash settlement. |
| Worked arithmetic | Recomputed the $105 call bought for $3: at a $112 stock price on expiration its net payoff is `(112 − 105) × 100 − 300 = $400`; at $106 it is `−$200`; break-even is $108. Recomputed the $95 put bought for $2: at $88 it returns `$500` after premium, and break-even is $93. A $2.10/$2.30 quote costs $20 for a one-contract immediate ask-to-bid round trip before fees. The seven-day plan totals 10 hours; 104 weeks × 10 hours is 1,040 hours. These are classroom expiration/payoff examples, not guaranteed broker outcomes. |
| Curriculum sequence | Same-expiration payoff exercises no longer include calendars: a calendar retains a live leg when the near leg expires. Calendars now appear after Greeks and term structure. The safety sprint now explicitly uses eight sourced scenarios plus two learner-generated cases. |
| Math prerequisites | [MIT 18.05](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-05-introduction-to-probability-and-statistics-spring-2022/pages/syllabus/) formally requires multivariable calculus. The course now starts beginner statistics with [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability), with MIT as a later extension. The 104-week target extends if foundational math needs work. |
| Coding prerequisites | [Python's official tutorial](https://docs.python.org/3/tutorial/) explicitly addresses people who already know programming. The course now starts non-programmers with [MIT 6.100L](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-100l-introduction-to-cs-and-programming-using-python-fall-2022/). |
| Account, assignment, and settlement | The [SEC account bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-77) supports broker-specific approval. [OIC](https://www.optionseducation.org/optionsoverview/exercising-options) and [FINRA](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/trading-options-understanding-assignment) support early assignment and a spread leg leaving stock exposure. The risk guide now distinguishes a short seller's last chance to **buy to close** from a long holder's broker exercise cutoff, states that a close order must fill, and limits the vertical credit-spread formula to matching standard one-to-one contracts. [Cboe specifications](https://www.cboe.com/tradable_products/sp_500/spx_weekly_options/specifications) confirm the SPX/SPXW/XSP versus SPY exercise and settlement distinctions used in the course. |
| 2026 margin and tax | [FINRA Notice 26-10](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-10) confirms the intraday margin change effective June 4, 2026 and broker phase-in through October 20, 2027; a universal $25,000 pattern-day-trader statement would be misleading during the transition. The risk guide requires broker verification. [IRS Publication 550](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550) is labeled as the available 2025 edition; its Section 1256 discussion is a reason to keep records and obtain individual tax advice, not a blanket classification of every index option. |
| Course and simulator catalog | Source descriptions were aligned to the providers' exact pages: [IBKR course catalog](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/campus/traders-academy/finance-courses/), [OIC tools](https://www.optionseducation.org/options-quotes-calculators), and [CME simulator FAQ](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/faqs/trading-simulator-faq.html). The library now states CME practice data are delayed and its simulator does not automatically settle open positions at expiry. [IBKR documents paper-fill limitations](https://www.ibkrguides.com/orgportal/aboutpapertradingaccount.htm); the research lab no longer treats simulated fills as live execution calibration. |
| Execution-cost research | One former citation did not support its claim. The lab now cites [Muravyev and Pearson](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/33/11/4973/5732665) for the difference between quoted and effective spreads and [de Silva, So, and Smith](https://academic.oup.com/rof/article-abstract/30/2/489/8301159) for high retail option costs around earnings. These findings do not supply a universal fill assumption. |
| Historical data and backtests | [Cboe DataShop's EOD product](https://datashop.cboe.com/option-eod-summary) supports 15:45 and end-of-day snapshots, with optional Greeks calculated at 15:45; fields, quote sizes, and underlying index licenses require product-specific checking. The [backtest-overfitting paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2326253) supports logging strategy selection and testing on untouched data. The [NBER option-premium paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31833) reports narrowing premia over the last 15 years of a sample ending in 2022; the course now states the sample boundary rather than implying a timeless or current trading edge. |

## Remaining limits

1. **Paid and account-gated content:** We verified public catalog and product descriptions, not Cboe's registered learning portal, an IBKR account, ORATS subscriptions, OptionMetrics, or a purchased Cboe historical dataset. Actual tools, fields, prices, and access can differ by account and license.
2. **Cboe historical coverage discrepancy:** [Cboe's product pages](https://datashop.cboe.com/option-eod-summary) and [FAQ](https://datashop.cboe.com/faqs) present different historical start dates. The course makes no start-date promise; confirm the exact symbol, dates, fields, and quote-size conventions with Cboe before purchase.
3. **Broker and personal circumstances:** We cannot verify a particular broker's current permissions, house margin, exercise cutoff, fills, fees, or the learner's tax treatment without account-specific information. Those checks are explicit gates in the course.
4. **Course effectiveness:** No public source can verify that this exact curriculum will make a particular person profitable or “the world's best.” The course's assessments measure knowledge, process, and research quality; live performance would need a long, independently checked record across market regimes.

These corrections describe the version reviewed at the time of this source audit. Later course expansions are recorded below. When a source changes, update the relevant claim and the checked date rather than relying on this report indefinitely.

## Teaching-volume addendum — October 26, 2026

After this source audit, the syllabus was expanded into [four teaching volumes](README.md) with **26 prose lessons, 104 specific weekly plans, exercises and answer keys, and a gate for every module** (about 25,000 words of teaching material). The beginner chapters were checked against SEC/OCC/OIC/FINRA/Cboe sources; the behavior chapters against OIC/Cboe/IBKR; and the quantitative chapters against Khan Academy, MIT, OCC, and Cboe. Both runnable Python examples in Volume 3 were executed and their outputs checked. The research volume received an independent review that corrected its BXM data-access example, defined slippage to avoid double counting, and made forward shadow logging continuous from week 85 onward. All local links in the expanded project resolve.

The exercises have not been trialed with a new learner. Their pass standards test understanding and process, not a demonstrated ability to earn live trading profits.

**Limits of this check:** The bundled prices, fills, P&L, and forecasts are invented and show only whether the code follows its stated assumptions. This revision did not buy historical contract data, access a live brokerage account, measure live execution, or trial the expanded course with a new learner. The prior external-source audit above describes its original scope; the new internal link and code checks are not a fresh verification of every external course, book, fee, or broker rule. Before a real product or data purchase, check current OCC, exchange, broker, and vendor information again. A completed synthetic or published-index capstone supplies no direct evidence that an executable options strategy will be profitable.
